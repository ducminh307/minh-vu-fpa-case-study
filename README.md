# FP&A Case Study --- Nordwerk Components GmbH

**Independent portfolio project \| Excel \| January--September 2026**\
**Important:** Nordwerk Components GmbH and all figures in this case
study are fictional. This is a self-directed learning project, not work
performed for an employer or client.

## Project objective

Build an integrated, Excel-based FP&A case study that compares six
months of actual financial performance with budget, analyses variances,
prepares a three-scenario Q3 forecast, and communicates the results
through a management dashboard and executive report.

## Business questions

-   How did revenue, direct costs, operating expenses and EBITDA perform
    against budget in January--June?
-   Which variances contributed to the EBITDA shortfall?
-   How could revenue growth, COGS ratio and operating expenses affect
    Q3 profitability?
-   What should management monitor, and what additional information is
    needed to validate the forecast?

## Tools and methods

-   Microsoft Excel: linked P&L model, variance analysis, forecast
    assumptions, scenario model, KPI summary and dashboard.
-   Financial analysis: actual vs budget, absolute and percentage
    variances, gross profit, EBITDA and EBITDA margin.
-   Scenario planning: Base, Optimistic and Downside cases for
    July--September 2026.
-   AI assistance: used as a learning and review aid; calculations and
    assumptions should be understood and validated by the project
    author.

## Key results --- January--June 2026

  KPI                        Actual       Budget   Variance
  -------------------- ------------ ------------ ----------
  Revenue                €2,700,000   €2,720,000   -€20,000
  COGS                   €1,616,000   €1,632,000   -€16,000
  Gross Profit           €1,084,000   €1,088,000    -€4,000
  Operating Expenses       €715,000     €697,000   +€18,000
  EBITDA                   €369,000     €391,000   -€22,000

Revenue was 0.7% below budget. COGS was €16,000 below budget, a
favourable cost variance. Operating expenses were €18,000 above budget,
an unfavourable variance. Gross Profit was €4,000 below budget, and
EBITDA finished €22,000 (5.6%) below budget.

The EBITDA margin was approximately 13.7% actual versus 14.4% budget, a
decrease of about 0.7 percentage points.

May had the largest monthly EBITDA shortfall (€11,000); June was €3,000
above budget.

## Q3 forecast scenarios

  Scenario       Q3 Revenue   Q3 EBITDA   EBITDA Margin
  ------------ ------------ ----------- ---------------
  Base           €1,468,992    €217,760           14.8%
  Optimistic     €1,498,372    €241,370           16.1%
  Downside       €1,432,812    €169,985           11.9%

The scenario range for Q3 EBITDA is approximately €170,000 to €241,000.
The scenarios are driven by different assumptions for monthly revenue
growth, COGS as a percentage of revenue, and operating expenses. They
are illustrative planning cases, not predictions or management guidance.

## Management recommendations

1.  Investigate May's €11,000 EBITDA shortfall by reviewing sales
    volumes, pricing, product mix, production output and one-off costs.
2.  Review the €18,000 Opex overspend by cost centre and distinguish
    recurring from one-off items.
3.  Understand why COGS was €16,000 below budget and test whether the
    savings are sustainable.
4.  Monitor revenue, COGS percentage, Opex and EBITDA monthly against
    the forecast.
5.  Refresh the forecast when actual sales pipeline, customer orders,
    supplier prices, production volumes and staffing plans are
    available.

## Workbook structure

-   `P&L_MODEL` --- six-month actual and budget P&L.
-   `VARIANCE_ANALYSIS` --- monthly actual-versus-budget variances.
-   `KPI_SUMMARY` --- six-month totals and interpretation.
-   `FORECAST_ASSUMPTIONS` --- scenario inputs.
-   `FORECAST_MODEL` --- July--September forecast calculations.
-   `SCENARIO_SUMMARY` --- Q3 scenario comparison.
-   `DASHBOARD` --- management dashboard.
-   `EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY` --- consolidated financial summary.
-   `DAY_1` to `DAY_5` guide sheets --- project workflow and
    instructions.

## Limitations

-   The company and all financial data are fictional.
-   Forecast assumptions are illustrative and have not been validated
    against a real sales pipeline, supplier contracts, production plan
    or staffing plan.
-   The simplified training P&L calculates EBITDA as Gross Profit less
    Operating Expenses. It does not model separate depreciation and
    amortisation, interest or tax lines.
-   EBITDA is not cash flow, net income or a statutory accounting
    measure.
-   No causal explanation is inferred where the dataset contains no
    supporting operational detail.
